GENERAL SAFETY RULES

Every employee must:

• Wear Personal Protective Equipment (PPE).
• Follow supervisor instructions.
• Keep workstations clean.
• Report unsafe conditions immediately.
• Never bypass machine safety guards.

ACCIDENT REPORTING

If an accident occurs:

1. Stop work immediately.
2. Inform your supervisor.
3. Call emergency response team.
4. Record incident in the Incident Register.
5. Do not restart equipment until authorized.
