DOCUMENT: Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I report an accident?

A:
Stop work, inform supervisor, contact safety officer, and complete an incident report.

Q: What PPE is required near molten metal?

A:
Helmet, face shield, heat resistant gloves, protective apron, and safety shoes.

Q: What should I do if the conveyor belt jams?

A:
Press Emergency Stop, disconnect power, remove obstruction, inspect rollers, and restart only after approval.

Q: How do I become a maintenance technician?

A:
Complete Preventive Maintenance, PLC Basics, Hydraulic Systems, and Lockout Tagout training.
