DOCUMENT: Conveyor Belt Preventive Maintenance

Section 1: Daily Inspection

- Inspect the conveyor belt for wear, cuts, or damage.
- Check belt alignment.
- Ensure the emergency stop button is functioning.
- Remove dust and debris from the belt and rollers.

Section 2: Weekly Maintenance

- Inspect roller bearings.
- Check belt tension.
- Tighten loose bolts and fasteners.
- Lubricate moving parts according to the maintenance schedule.

Section 3: Safety Precautions

- Stop the conveyor before maintenance.
- Disconnect electrical power.
- Apply Lockout/Tagout procedures.
- Never clean or adjust a moving conveyor belt.
- Wear appropriate PPE during maintenance.

Section 4: Reporting

- Report excessive wear, unusual noise, belt slippage, or damaged rollers to the maintenance supervisor.
- Record all maintenance activities in the maintenance log.
