DOCUMENT: Preventive Maintenance SOP

DAILY

• Clean equipment
• Check lubrication
• Remove dust

WEEKLY

• Inspect belts
• Tighten bolts
• Test sensors

MONTHLY

• Replace worn parts
• Verify calibration
• Update maintenance log

LOCKOUT TAGOUT

Before maintenance:

1. Disconnect power.
2. Lock power source.
3. Attach warning tag.
4. Verify zero energy.
5. Begin work.
