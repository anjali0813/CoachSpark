DOCUMENT: Emergency Response Guide

FIRE

1. Raise alarm.
2. Use fire extinguisher if trained.
3. Evacuate safely.
4. Report to assembly point.

ELECTRIC SHOCK

1. Disconnect power.
2. Do not touch victim directly.
3. Call emergency response.
4. Begin CPR if trained.

CHEMICAL SPILL

1. Isolate area.
2. Wear PPE.
3. Inform safety officer.
4. Follow spill procedure.
