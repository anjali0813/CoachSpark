DOCUMENT: Electrical Safety Guidelines

CATEGORY: Safety

Always inspect electrical cables before use.

Never touch exposed wires.

Use insulated gloves when working on electrical systems.

Turn OFF the main power before maintenance.

Only authorized electricians may repair electrical equipment.

If someone receives an electric shock:

1. Disconnect power immediately.
2. Do not touch the victim until power is isolated.
3. Call emergency response.
4. Begin CPR if trained.
